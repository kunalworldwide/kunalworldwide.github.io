The era of unconstrained AI coding spend is ending. One enterprise famously vaporized a $500M AI budget in 30 days; another exhausted its annual AI budget by April after rolling out coding tools to 5,000 engineers.

This talk introduces **Kimchi**, an open-source autonomous coding agent that combines a multi-model coding harness, inference cloud, and spend governance console in one terminal-first platform. Built by Cast AI and graduated to GA in July 2026, Kimchi routes tasks across specialized models (planner, builder, reviewer, explorer, researcher, judge) so the right model does the right work — cutting token costs by 40–60% while matching or beating proprietary models on benchmarks.

Key topics covered:

- Why AI coding is not SaaS: utility-based token pricing and the mechanics of runaway spend
- The Kimchi harness: planning, building, reviewing, and grading work with specialized subagents
- Ferment workflows: give the agent a project, not a task, and resume across sessions
- Multi-model routing economics and real-world cost savings
- Spend governance: org, team, API key, and user-level hard caps with auto-termination
- Data sovereignty and self-hosted deployment options
